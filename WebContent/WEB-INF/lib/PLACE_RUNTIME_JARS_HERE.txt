WebContent/WEB-INF/lib/ — Runtime JAR Dependencies
====================================================
JARs placed here are automatically bundled inside the WAR file
and loaded by Tomcat at runtime.

REQUIRED:
  mysql-connector-java-8.0.33.jar  (copy from project root lib/ folder)

DO NOT place servlet-api.jar here — Tomcat provides it; including it
causes class-loading conflicts.
